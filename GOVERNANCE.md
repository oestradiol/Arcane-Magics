# Governance

Repository authority is typed, scoped, and revisable.

## Separation of roles

```text
GENERATE != EVALUATE != AUTHORIZE != EXECUTE != REALITY != VERIFY
```

A maintainer, model, paper, compiler stage, CI check, or successful implementation does not become Truth Sovereign by occupying a useful role.

## Candidate lifecycle

1. identify object, frame, register, index, jurisdiction, source, and relevant history;
2. state the claim or transformation;
3. evaluate warrant separately from governance;
4. preserve alternatives and residuals when unresolved;
5. authorize only within declared scope;
6. require receipts for claimed execution/publication;
7. verify against the target claim rather than neighboring prose;
8. write back only dependency-local consequences.

## Three non-identical ternaries

Developmental/root research:

```text
|V> → |Δ> → |T>
```

Epistemic warrant over an already formed proposition:

```text
LICENSE_F(P) | LICENSE_F(not-P) | UNDETERMINED_F(P)
```

Governance over an already typed candidate:

```text
ADMIT | WITHHOLD | REJECT/CUT
```

No serialization between these coordinates is automatic.

## Corrections and contributions

Criticism, counterexamples, source corrections, failed replications, negative results, and reconstruction failures are first-class returned consequence. A merged pull request is not Canon by virtue of merge. A rejected route retains provenance sufficient to reconstruct why it was cut.

## Maintainer authority

Maintainers may route, review, authorize repository mutations, and steward releases. Those roles do not license hidden participant states, scientific claims, theological claims, or ontological conclusions outside their supporting bridges.
