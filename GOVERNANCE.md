# Governance

Suppose a model proposes a change, a validator accepts its syntax, and the change executes successfully. None of those events answers whether the proposal was warranted, authorized, legitimate, or verified against Reality. Venus × Minerva keeps those relations separate because collapsing them changes what can correct the system.

```text
GENERATE != EVALUATE != AUTHORIZE != EXECUTE != REALITY != VERIFY
```

## Candidate lifecycle

1. recover the referent, frame, register, index, jurisdiction, source, and relevant history;
2. state the candidate claim or transformation;
3. evaluate epistemic warrant separately from governance;
4. preserve alternatives and residuals when unresolved;
5. authorize only within declared scope;
6. execute only through bounded capability;
7. retain receipts for what actually happened;
8. verify the target claim rather than neighboring prose or build success;
9. write back only the dependencies the returned consequence changes.

A declared caution is not yet a working fence. The enforcing transition, check, receipt, or counterfactual test must be identifiable. See [Referent-first authoring](knowledge/method/REFERENT_FIRST_AUTHORING.md), [Executed-fence failure](historical-regressions/REGRESSION_LEDGER.md), and [Returned consequence](knowledge/problems/RETURNED_CONSEQUENCE_AND_OTHER.md).

## Three non-identical ternaries

Developmental/root research:

```text
|V> → |Δ> → |T>
```

Epistemic warrant over an already formed proposition:

```text
LICENSE_F(P) | LICENSE_F(not-P) | UNDETERMINED_F(P)
```

Governance over an already typed candidate:

```text
ADMIT | WITHHOLD | REJECT/CUT
```

No serialization among these coordinates is automatic. `UNDETERMINED` is not `WITHHOLD`; `ADMIT` is not unrestricted truth; `CUT` does not delete history.

## Corrections and contributions

Criticism, counterexamples, source corrections, failed replications, negative results, and reconstruction failures are first-class returned consequence. A merged pull request is not Canon by virtue of merge. A rejected route retains enough provenance to reconstruct why it was cut.

Where intellectual ancestry matters, use typed credit rather than citation proximity. See [Credit relations](knowledge/credit/CREDIT_RELATIONS.md) and [Comparative subtraction](knowledge/method/COMPARATIVE_SUBTRACTION.md).

## Maintainer authority

Maintainers may route, review, authorize repository mutations, and steward releases. Those roles do not license claims about another participant's hidden state, scientific mechanisms, theological truth, or ontology outside the bridges that support them.
