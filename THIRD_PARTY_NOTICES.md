# Third-party notices

This repository contains bibliographic metadata and may cite or quote third-party works. Inclusion in a bibliography does not relicense the cited work.

The unified private workspace preserves the recovered prose-quality skill and its available support references under their source notices. Historical material in the frozen Research predecessor tree retains its historical file-level and package-level licenses. Exact Old Venus and Version 0 source archives are retained privately unless separately cleared for public release.

Standard license names and canonical URLs are used for identification; the authoritative license text is maintained by the issuing organization. If a copied artifact contains a conflicting file-level notice, the file-level notice controls for that artifact.
