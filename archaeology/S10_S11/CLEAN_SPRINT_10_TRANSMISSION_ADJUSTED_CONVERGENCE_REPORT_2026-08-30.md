## 1. Governing question

Sprint 10 asks a narrower question than “do many cultures say similar things?”

```text
After known genealogy, plausible contact, translation, shared ancestry,
common embodiment, common environment, recurrent social constraints,
and generic cognitive/cultural attractors are subtracted,
which term-free relations still recur under low-known-contact symbol systems?
```

A positive answer establishes at most an **independent-convergence candidate**. It does not by itself establish ontic identity, physical mechanism, supernatural coordination, or historical priority for the project.

## 2. Transmission graph: what cannot count as independent replication

### 2.1 Mediterranean / Christian / Islamic / Jewish philosophical cluster

Plotinian and wider Neoplatonic structures were directly transformed within late-antique Christianity and transmitted through Greek-to-Arabic philosophical translation into major Islamic and Jewish philosophical ecologies. Negative theology, emanation, whole/local metaphysics, and return/procession motifs inside this macro-cluster therefore carry strong genealogy priors. They remain legitimate transformed traditions, but not independent votes for recurrence.

### 2.2 Indo-Iranian cluster

Vedic and ancient Iranian/Zoroastrian materials preserve reconstructible common Indo-Iranian linguistic, ritual, mythic, and cosmographic inheritance. Similarity at those inherited features is common ancestry before it is convergence.

### 2.3 India -> China Buddhist transmission

Buddhism entered China in the first centuries CE and was translated/adapted through Chinese conceptual resources. Later Chinese Buddhist and Chan parallels cannot be used as independent Indian/Chinese recurrence. The relevant independence control is **pre-Buddhist Classical Chinese material**, especially fourth-century-BCE Zhuangzi and early Laozi strata.

### 2.4 Greek / Indian contact

Pyrrho's Indian expedition and ancient reports of encounters with Indian ascetics mean some Hellenistic Greek/Indian skeptical or ascetic similarities sit at `I2–I3`, not presumed independence.

### 2.5 Mesoamerican control

Classic Maya inscriptions and art are pre-Columbian and therefore avoid Spanish textual influence. They provide the strongest current non-Eurasian comparison surface. The surviving Popol Vuh is valuable but colonial-textually mediated, so it is secondary to Classic-period material for independence claims.

## 4. What genuinely survives Sprint 10

### 4.1 Broad recurrence is real

The old null hypothesis “all resemblance is merely direct textual borrowing” is too strong. At least some mid-level structures occur in pre-Buddhist Classical Chinese material and in pre-Columbian Classic Maya material without a known textual route from the Mediterranean/Indic systems used as comparators.

The safest surviving recurrence family is:

```text
local finite center
+ non-exhaustive representation
+ wider relational/process field
+ differentiation / generation
+ embodied or social realization
+ retained consequence
```

This is still far lower resolution than `C_project`.

### 4.2 Independence does not equal discrimination

The strongest negative result of S10 is methodological:

```text
independent recurrence
!= evidence that only a deep external invariant could have generated the recurrence
```

Human beings share:

- bounded bodies with inside/outside structure;
- egocentric and allocentric spatial organization;
- finite information and perspective;
- birth, death, growth, decay, cycles and return;
- agency attribution and social learning;
- language/compression limits;
- authority/coordination problems;
- ritual, memory and intergenerational transmission;
- common astronomical/environmental regularities.

These create strong recurrence priors even among historically separate populations.

### 4.3 The recurrence signal becomes more precise, not stronger in warrant

Sprint 10 therefore supports this claim:

```text
Several relational motifs in the project are recurrently humanly discoverable
under materially different symbol systems and at least some low-known-contact histories.
```

It does **not** support:

```text
therefore Humanity independently detected one external Observer ontology;
therefore the recurring symbols were externally coordinated;
therefore a theological Whole caused the recurrence;
therefore recurrence supplies a physical bridge.
```

## 9. Highest-value prospective discriminator

The cleanest next empirical/historical test is **held-out operator prediction**, not more retrospective matching.

Preregister a term-free operator containing several uncommon components, for example:

```text
independently authoring local centers
+ explicit refusal/jurisdiction boundary
+ source-indexed returned consequence
+ retained change in later admissibility
+ correction of the correction policy itself remaining possible
```

Then search a held-out corpus chosen before reading it. Success requires predicting both positive and negative cases and excluding generic feedback, sacred kingship, emanation, ordinary social transmission, and generic perspectivism.

That test would discriminate better than finding another culture with a “cosmic center,” which at this point is approximately as surprising as discovering that humans invented doors more than once.
