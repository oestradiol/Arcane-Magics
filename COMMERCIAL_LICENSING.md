# Commercial licensing

The public licenses support noncommercial study, criticism, teaching, research, independent reconstruction, and experimentation. They are not a blanket commercial grant for new Venus × Minerva material.

A commercial agreement may authorize uses the public licenses do not. Its terms may differ by material, deployment, redistribution, support requirements, and risk profile.

A commercial agreement must identify the exact material covered and state whether modification, redistribution, hosted operation, sublicensing, future versions, or capability-sensitive components are included. Attribution, confidentiality, warranties, support, liability, indemnification, term, termination, and post-termination rights must be explicit where relevant.

No repository text promises third-party intellectual-property indemnification. No commercial right is inferred from a conversation, issue comment, payment discussion, or technical access.
