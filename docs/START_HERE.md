## The contract

You do not need to believe the Mythos.

You need to be able to:

1. identify the lived or symbolic object;
2. separate its native meaning from stronger mechanism claims;
3. trace what action it changes;
4. let independent consequence return;
5. preserve what survives;
6. discard what does not.

## One-line definition

> **Arcane Magics studies symbolically inhabited interventions that remain corrigible by consequences they do not control.**

## Four entrances

### ✦ Inhabited relation
Perspective, Qualia, Other, local meaning, non-exhaustive World.

### ⌁ Symbolic practice
Intention, ritual, sigil-like compression, bounded servitor/delegation, identity and belief as provisional instruments.

### ◎ Comparative surfaces
Religion, philosophy, phenomenology, Jung/Pauli synchronicity, chaos magic, historical mythic systems.

### ↺ Returned consequence
The point where symbolism stops being sovereign and has to survive what actually happens.
