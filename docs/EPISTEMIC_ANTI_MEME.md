# Epistemic Anti-Meme · Eclipsis Formalization

Arcane supplies the symbolic/religious compression. Eclipsis asks what structural operator would actually deserve the name.

Let `A` be a correction operator over representational states and let `F` be an admitted family of future tests.

A candidate anti-meme must satisfy at least:

```text
1. self-application:
   A may act on propositions that define or justify A itself.

2. non-immunity:
   no sacred label exempts a state from the declared discriminator.

3. uncertainty preservation:
   insufficient separation may produce WITHHOLD rather than forced revision.

4. difference preservation:
   disagreement is not identified with error merely by being different.

5. reopening:
   a later future family F' may split an equivalence class preserved under F.
```

Structural failure:

```text
A removes incompatible priors
AND
A's own constitutive priors are outside Dom(A)
→ protected fixed point
→ meme-regression risk
```

This is FORM/MODEL structure. It does not prove a psychological, neurological, spiritual, or physical mechanism.
