# Prose standard

The Venus × Minerva reconstruction was edited against a recovered prose-quality corpus retained in the unified private workspace. The public rule is simpler than shipping an editing toolkit as live research infrastructure.

- define or distinguish something in every technical paragraph;
- prefer exact outcomes over significance language;
- separate structural claims from empirical or physical claims;
- do not invent numeric precision;
- state a counterargument when it changes the warrant;
- preserve historical date/version/status headers when they identify an audit object;
- do not mechanically replace wording when the replacement changes causal direction or authorship;
- use automated scans as triage, then read prose manually.

Formal notation is not evidence. Fluency is not warrant. A clean sentence should make a dependency easier to inspect, not harder to notice.
