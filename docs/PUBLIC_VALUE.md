## 30 seconds

**Venus-Minerva is an experimental developmental-intelligence architecture in which answers, representations, research obligations, evidence dependencies, failures, and parts of the learning procedure itself become persistent, reconstructible machine state.**

The engineering target is simple to say and difficult to fake:

> let the learner increasingly choose and modify how it learns without letting it manufacture the external evidence that certifies those modifications.

## What is unusual

The candidate contribution is the executable composition:

```text
persistent representation
→ learner-owned inquiry/development
→ explicit evidence dependencies
→ non-preauthored return
→ claim-local audit
→ retained PASS / FAIL / WITHHOLD
→ successor
→ possible revision of revision machinery
```

with:

```text
learner authors more of development
AND
learner does not become sovereign over the answer
```

- reconstructible persistent runtime state;
- refinement when a later future-test family reopens an erased distinction;

- evidence-role and evidence-budget selection;
- open-domain problem selection;
- self-curriculum target generation;
- preregistered developmental gates;
- learner-owned World-feed query policy;
- preserved negative branches;
- STOP/WITHHOLD under insufficient evidence;
- newer admitted governance infrastructure without false promotion of the developmental head.

## The failure that best explains the design

EDU17 initially looked successful and was rejected after claim-local provenance inspection. EDU17R1 then stopped before the intended repair test because the new return exposed a prior semantic defect:

```text
mention
!=
incidence
```

That sequence matters more than a flattering benchmark screenshot. The architecture is designed so a local success can still become a permanent negative if a required conjunct fails.

## What belongs to siblings

Those branches may constrain Venus. Their warrant does not automatically become engineering evidence.
