## Why it exists

Some of the project’s hardest questions are not purely formal and not purely symbolic. They concern how a finite local perspective can inhabit a structured field, remain distinct from what surrounds it, receive consequence across a boundary, and change without dissolving its own index.

Eclipsis is where those structural questions are allowed to meet:

- polyhedral local views;
- path-sensitive gluing;
- meta-qualia;
- consciousness bridge questions;
- General Quantum Theory / LQG relation;
- PPD-Crystal and horizon Residual hypotheses;
- cosmological-flow projections.
