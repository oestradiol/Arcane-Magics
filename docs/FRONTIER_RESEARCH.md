# Frontier Research — Eclipsis

Eclipsis asks which mathematical/logical structures remain after project-specific vocabulary, carrier assumptions, or symbolic decoration are removed.

## Main programs

### Meta-Dynamics

Test whether the proposed recurrence contributes an invariant not already supplied by mature alternatives. If deletion/substitution preserves all relevant future consequences, reduce it.

### RegisterBridge / cross-register structure

Formalize what is preserved when a relation moves between symbolic, formal, executable, model, and scientific registers. Parent ancestry and structural resemblance do not transfer warrant.

### Returnable closure

Study open composition where closure remains permeable to non-preauthored return.

```text
closure != isolation
open bridge != arbitrary permeability
returned distinction != preauthored prediction
```

### Consciousness / meta-qualia

The structural organization of locally inhabited differentiation is a research object. The physical carrier/mechanism bridge remains OPEN.

### Quantum-gravity bridge

Polyhedral/LQG/general-quantum correspondences remain candidate structural bridges until explicit physical discriminators return.

## Method

Use mature-substitution tests, formal counterexamples, source removal, proof obligations, and explicit carrier maps. Route engineering consequences to Minerva and future/empirical tests to OFE.
