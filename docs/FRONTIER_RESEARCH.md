# Frontier Research — Arcane Magics

This is the live frontier of the Religion / past / inhabited-symbolic branch.

## Core research question

What useful distinctions can symbolic, religious, mythic, ritual, aesthetic, and phenomenological organization expose while remaining corrigible by consequences they do not control?

## Programs

### Symbolic intervention

Represent an intervention as a bounded object:

```text
<intention, symbol, local state, scope, jurisdiction, expiry, return>
```

Test whether the symbolic organization changes human interpretation/action in reproducible bounded ways without upgrading that effect into unsupported external mechanism.

### Synchronicity / meaningful coincidence

Keep three objects separate:

```text
meaningful coincidence
statistical anomaly
causal/acausal mechanism claim
```

A strong interpretation must survive hostile readings, source removal, and alternative models.

### Astrology

Current status: admitted as symbolic celestial indexing.

Open outward questions:
- does any claimed predictive effect survive prefrozen evaluation? → OFE;
- is there a nontrivial structural invariant worth formalizing? → Eclipsis;
- can a bounded symbolic intervention be tested behaviorally? → Minerva;
- can interpretation remain permeable to non-preauthored return? → Venus.

### Anti-meme / anti-scripture

The branch must apply its own critique to itself.

A vocabulary that only appears powerful while retained, and collapses under term removal, should be treated as scaffold rather than invariant.

## Evaluation style

Prefer:
- source-removal;
- hostile reader reconstruction;
- cross-tradition transfer;
- explicit alternative explanations;
- prefrozen outward discriminators.

Do not use internal resonance as independent evidence.
