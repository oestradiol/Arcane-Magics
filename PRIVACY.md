# Privacy

Public research should stand without private personal-source material.

Do not commit private messages, nonpublic participant identities, intimate or medical details, financial or household information, private contact details, precise home or routine locations, unpublished identifying trajectories, unnecessary third-party personal data, or speculation presented as fact about another person's hidden state.

When a private case motivates a general result, publish the least-identifying formulation that preserves the research distinction. If a claim cannot retain its warrant after private material is removed, keep the claim private or mark it unresolved.

Raw private archives and internal provenance stores are not part of this repository.
