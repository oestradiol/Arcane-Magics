# Privacy boundary

The public repository must be reconstructible without private personal-source material.

Do not commit by default private participant identifiers, nonpublic messages, relationship or intimacy details, health or financial information, private contact details, precise home/routine locations, sexual history, unpublished identifying trajectories, speculation about another person's hidden state, or unnecessary third-party personal data.

When a private case teaches a general lesson, export the least-identifying relation that preserves the research distinction. If public warrant cannot survive removal of private material, the claim stays private or withheld.

Raw historical archives and the exact private Canonical workspace remain outside the public tree unless separately reviewed for release. Historical usefulness is not a privacy waiver.

Privacy review covers reachable public history as well as the checked-out tree.
