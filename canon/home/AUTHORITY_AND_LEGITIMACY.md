# Authority and legitimacy

Authority is indexed by object, register, source, jurisdiction, and task.

## Projection authority

- **CBRD** has authority only over what its epistemic/formal/scientific premises, models, measurements, discriminators, and evidence license.
- **Arcane Magics** may state independently licensed PHEN, THEO, REL/STRUCT, symbolic, and model-ontological objects at their native scope.
- Neither may convert a missing bridge into sovereignty over the other.
- Only an explicit RegisterBridge may transfer consequence across registers, and the bridge must state what warrant changes, what does not, and what loss occurs.

## Participant authority

A participant is not a sensor owned by the project. Their current experience, consent, refusal, interpretation, and boundary have first-person jurisdiction at the participant index. Models of another participant remain models.

```text
Other != model(Other)
prediction != authorization
causal influence != authorship
```

## Higher-order systems

Distributed cognition, institutions, collectives, or higher-order control may acquire real causal organization without thereby acquiring unrestricted authorization over their constituents.

```text
higher-order functional organization
!= unitary phenomenal subject
!= automatic authority over constituent Humans
```

## Rights as transformation constraints

Criticism, correction, refusal, mutation, fork, exit, and source-local authorship are protected channels where the relevant Human/participant index applies. A system that disables those channels to preserve itself becomes less legitimate even if its local predictive or operational capability improves.
