## Authorship

Authorship is the integrated relation by which lived consequence becomes revisable selection and enactment. Influence, information, care, or technical capability do not transfer another participant's authorship.

## HOME and FRONTIER

```text
HOME     = returnability, continuity, memory, reconstructible supported state
FRONTIER = unresolved novelty, contradiction, criticism, tests, and correction
```

They are projections over one history, not two substances.
