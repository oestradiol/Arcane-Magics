# Projection model: Arcane Magics, CBRD, HOME and FRONTIER

The project has two independent axes. Conflating them destroys information.

## Arcane Magics

```text
Arcane Magics
= Inhabited THEO–PHEN
  ↔ Corrigible Ontological Articulation
```

Arcane is not “the philosophy side.” It is the inhabited projection in which phenomenal occurrence, embodiment, interpretation, theological whole-orientation, alterity, symbolic rendering, and explicit ontology remain in reciprocal correction.

Its epistemic discipline is internal, not outsourced to CBRD. Arcane claims must state their register and bridge status; vividness, salience, coherence, symbolism, or theological adequacy do not silently become empirical mechanism.

## CBRD

```text
CBRD
= Consequence-Bearing Relational Dynamics
= epistemic–formal–operational projection
```

CBRD emphasizes explicit objects, typed relations, model comparison, mathematical formalization, operationalization, evidence, countermodels, discriminators, verification, and negative results. It may use Gödel, Löb, fixed-point, logical uncertainty, or self-reference results where the specific argument requires them; “Gödelian” is not the project's global epistemic brand.

## Reciprocal relation

```text
Arcane claim
→ CBRD may REPRESENT / CONSTRAIN / COMPARE / LOCALIZE missing bridge premises
-/-> CBRD derives Arcane interpretation

CBRD model/result
→ Arcane may inhabit / interpret / contextualize / expose meaning-loss or register mismatch
-/-> Arcane supplies scientific warrant by interpretation alone
```

Neither projection is the other's wrapper, proof layer, or truth-sovereign core.

## Cross-axis rule

```text
HOME↔FRONTIER = temporal/functional continuity-correction axis
Arcane↔CBRD    = reciprocal register/projection/audit axis
```

Both projections contain HOME and FRONTIER functions. `Arcane=HOME` and `CBRD=FRONTIER` are rejected collapses.

## Publication

A paper is a frozen projection from the living project, not the authority root. Arcane Magics and CBRD may be published separately, revised at different rates, and criticized by different communities while remaining reciprocal views of one endogenous genealogy.
