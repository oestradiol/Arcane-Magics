# Public research frontier

This is the public router for unresolved work. `OPEN` means the current frame does not license closure. `WITHHOLD` means a candidate is retained but not promoted. `RETYPE_IF_NO_DISCRIMINATOR` means keep the relation only if it changes a real reconstruction, prediction, transport, or decision consequence.

Status: `GENUINE_OPEN`

### F02 — first consequential differentiation δ

Status: `GENUINE_OPEN / HIGH PRIORITY`

```text
vacuous_relation --δ?--> |Δ> --stabilize--> |T>
```

A symmetry-preserving deterministic transition cannot break a fully symmetric root at the declared symmetry index. Candidate mechanisms must state where asymmetry, stochasticity, boundary condition, instability, index, or equivalent structure enters.

### F12 — recursive sufficiency

Status: `GENUINE_OPEN`

Find the smallest representation sufficient for a declared future transformation family/horizon. If two histories compress to the same present representation but later admissible behavior differs, the quotient was not recursively sufficient.

### F13/F14/F15 — AntiGrammar / RegisterBridge / Compiler factorization

Status: `GENUINE_OPEN`

- `AntiGrammar_T`: consequential invariant/equivalence class under a declared transformation family.
- `RegisterBridge`: governed transport between typed registers.
- `Compiler`: broader staged transformation/obligation/lowering relation.

Open: composition, reversal, loss/fiber behavior, warrant transformation, jurisdiction, dependency-local failure, evidence-lineage obligations, and whether enriched bidirectional-transformation/contract/effect formalisms reproduce the required behavior with less bespoke machinery.

### F19 — polynomial-section obstruction / P vs NP specialization

Status: `GENUINE_OPEN / SPECIALIZATION OF F30`

Witness verification/construction is modeled as projection vs resource-bounded section. Structural nonidentity does not establish a complexity lower bound. A real argument requires an independently characterized obstruction invariant stable under the encodings/reductions used.

## Empirical / engineering frontier

### R01 — RegenerativeAccess

Operationalize whether a retained trace can still participate in future admissibility, judgment, action, reconstruction, criticism, mutation, or transmission.

### R02/R03 — semantic poisoning / Cryo capture

Discriminate ordinary forgetting, drift, hostile reinterpretation, aversion, and persistent representation that blocks recovery of the generative invariant. Measure when representation survives while correction/mutation/reconstruction becomes causally dead.

### R04 — Destructor → Cryo transition

Identify when a local persistence/protective constraint becomes system-wide self-protecting jurisdiction and suppresses the correction channels needed to revise it.

### R06 — source/founder removal

Test reconstruction, criticism, mutation, refusal, fork, exit, provenance, and useful consequence after founder/source wording removal.

### R08 — developmental research agent

Prospective engineering only. Test persistent learning/write-back, provenance, external-return robustness, authority separation, model drift, transfer, bounded self-modification, founder ablation, and maintenance burden without promoting the system to personhood or truth authority.

## Historical / provenance frontier

### H01 — long-term recurrence source

Status: `GENUINE_OPEN`

Repeated structural motifs across historical strata are compatible with cognitive continuity, implicit memory, shared problem structure, later influence, partial re-derivation, or combinations. Current evidence does not uniquely identify the source.

### H03 — operator-level transmission across historical strata

Status: `NARROWED OPEN / SOURCE GAP CLOSED`

Broad chronology is recorded. Successor archaeology has recovered a substantial primary RGM corpus, so RGM source availability is no longer the unresolved variable. See [RGM operator archaeology](../../knowledge/evidence/RGM_OPERATOR_ARCHAEOLOGY.md).

The remaining problem is operator-by-operator: actual exposure, forgotten influence, regression, re-derivation, independent convergence, or formal inheritance. Historical priority is not project origin, and chronology alone does not choose among those transmission models.
