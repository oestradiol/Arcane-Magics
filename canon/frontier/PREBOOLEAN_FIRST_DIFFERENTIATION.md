# Pre-Boolean first differentiation

The foundational target is narrower than “derive Being from Nothing.” It asks whether a relation-first root candidate can support a first nontrivial consequential differentiation without inserting the target cut into the premises.

```text
vacuous_relation --δ?--> |Δ> --σ?--> |T> --β--> local Boolean predicates
```

A Boolean valuation already needs a proposition domain. A three-valued epistemic valuation preserves unresolved propositions but also begins after proposition formation. Neither supplies `δ`.

A useful scoped obstruction follows from symmetry. If a root state `v` is fixed under a declared symmetry family `G`, and deterministic `δ` is `G`-equivariant, then `δ(v)` is also `G`-fixed. A symmetry-breaking account of first differentiation must therefore expose where symmetry is lost: latent root structure, the law, coupling, branching/actualization, or another independently specified mechanism.

Branching is one live model family: the space of possible branches may remain symmetric while an actual branch becomes differentiated. That observation does not identify the formalism with quantum mechanics or any physical collapse theory.
