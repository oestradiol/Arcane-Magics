# Licensing policy

This repository is the first public release boundary for Venus × Minerva and the project-owned predecessor material deliberately reborn into it. Historical internal license notices are provenance of prior intent; they are not surviving public grants because those predecessor repositories and manuscript packages were not published or distributed as public releases.

This file maps repository paths to controlling licenses. The complete license texts are vendored under `LICENSES/`.

## Theory, Canon, documentation, knowledge, and manuscripts

Unless a tracked file carries a more specific valid notice, original project-owned literary and knowledge material published in this repository is licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)**.

This includes, by default:

- `canon/`;
- `knowledge/`;
- `archaeology/` public-safe reconstructions;
- `docs/`;
- `papers/` manuscript sources and project-authored prose;
- root documentation such as `README.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, and `CHANGELOG.md`.

Canonical text: `LICENSES/CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.txt`.

## Software

Unless a tracked file carries a more specific valid notice, original project-owned executable software published in this repository is licensed under **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0**.

This includes, by default:

- `crates/`;
- `python/` project software and executable research tooling;
- `tools/`;
- executable code under `experiments/`;
- project-owned CI/build scripts where copyright applies.

Canonical text: `LICENSES/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.md`.

Because the commercial restriction is intentional, software under this policy is source-available/noncommercial rather than OSI Open Source.

## Clean reset of internal predecessor notices

No Venus × Minerva predecessor repository or manuscript package is treated as an earlier public distribution. Accordingly:

```text
historical internal license notice
!= public distribution
!= prior public grant
```

Project-owned material deliberately migrated from those internal predecessors receives its public license from this repository's current path/type policy when it is first published here. The archaeology may preserve old license text as historical evidence without giving that text present licensing authority.

## Third-party material

This policy can license only rights controlled by the relevant copyright holder. External publications, datasets, standards, code, figures, quotations beyond applicable exceptions, trademarks, and other third-party material remain subject to their own legal status and terms. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Commercial permissions

The relevant copyright holder may grant separate commercial permission for project-owned material. A separate commercial agreement can authorize uses the public noncommercial licenses do not; it does not cancel the public license already offered for the current released material.

No warranty, indemnification, exclusivity, service level, or endorsement is promised unless a signed agreement says so.

## Contributions

External contributions do not silently transfer commercial relicensing rights. Contributions to a commercially licensable core require an explicit reviewed rights arrangement before commercial relicensing can be assumed. Issues, criticism, test cases, and proposed patches do not by themselves transfer copyright.

## Release governance

Licensing answers who may exercise copyright-controlled rights. It is not a safety mechanism and does not decide what strategically sensitive capability or future-plan information should be published. Capability-sensitive or strategically WITHHOLD material remains governed by release policy before it enters the public repository.
