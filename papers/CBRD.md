# Consequence-Bearing Relational Dynamics (CBRD)

**Status:** manuscript in development; not yet published.

CBRD is the epistemic–formal–operational publication projection of Venus × Minerva. It develops explicit objects, typed relations, transformations, provenance, warrant, governance separation, model comparison, formal obligations, empirical discriminators, negative results, and reconstruction/verification tests.

CBRD should stand on definitions, scoped claims, evidence, countermodels, and independently inspectable references without borrowing warrant from Arcane Magics.

The project may use Gödel, Löb, diagonalization, fixed-point results, logical uncertainty, embedded agency, or related self-reference tools where a particular formal question requires them. None of those names serves as a blanket epistemic identity for CBRD.
