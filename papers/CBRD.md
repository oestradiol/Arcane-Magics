# Consequence-Bearing Relational Dynamics (CBRD)

**Status:** working manuscript; not yet published.

CBRD starts from a narrower problem than a general ontology: which represented or encountered differences can still change a system's later organization, and where can that causal transport fail?

CBRD is the **epistemic-formal-operational** publication projection of Venus × Minerva. It develops explicit objects, typed relations, provenance, warrant, governance separation, model comparison, formal obligations, empirical discriminators, negative results, and reconstruction/verification tests.
