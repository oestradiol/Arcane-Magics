\begin{abstract}
\emph{Arcane Magics} is the inhabited THEO--PHEN $\leftrightarrow$ corrigible-ontological-articulation projection of a larger research program about consequence-bearing relations. It begins from a modest asymmetry: lived occurrence is directly available at an inhabited perspective, while any explicit account of what that occurrence is remains mediated, revisable, and incomplete. The paper therefore distinguishes the local history-bearing center from the relation by which a world is presented to that center; distinguishes present perspective from diachronic observer; and treats theological orientation, phenomenological occurrence, and epistemic articulation as non-sovereign renderings of one evolving Observer--World relation. A relation-first root candidate is retained, but the first nontrivial differentiation remains open. The strongest earned mid-level claim is narrower: once consequential difference exists, it may cross a provisional boundary, be transformed or retained, and alter later admissible transformation. From this relation the paper develops Authorship, HOME/FRONTIER, reconstructive Spirit transport, semantic poisoning, Cryo capture, higher-order field transformation, and a source-removal criterion for publication. Historical traditions are credited for priority where they developed overlapping structures; project-causal genealogy is recorded separately. No symbolic or theological name supplies scientific warrant by adjacency. The manuscript is designed to become less necessary as readers acquire the ability to reconstruct, criticize, rename, and transfer its distinctions without founder rescue.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} inhabited theophenomenology; perspective; perspectivalization; observer; authorship; regenerative consequence; register bridge; comparative philosophy; reader autonomy.

\section{The problem is already inhabited}

Before any project vocabulary, consider three ordinary facts. A person can be modeled accurately without becoming identical to the model. A room is encountered as a place in which one can move and act before it becomes an inventory of propositions. A plan formed from a model can change the situation that later supplies evidence about the model.

The manuscript begins from the non-identities
\begin{equation}
\text{experience}\neq\text{model},\qquad
\text{model}\neq\text{Reality},\qquad
\text{Other}\neq\text{model(Other)}.
\end{equation}
These are conceptual separations, not numerical inequalities. They block a familiar error in recursive systems: a successful representation is promoted into authority over what it represents.

Situated and perspectival approaches in phenomenology and philosophy of science provide strong historical neighbors without defining this project \cite{Heidegger2026BeingTime,SEPHeidegger2025,Massimi2022Perspectival}. Process, enactive, cybernetic, and semiotic traditions likewise occupy much of the surrounding territory \cite{Whitehead1978Process,MaturanaVarela1980Autopoiesis,VonFoerster2003Understanding,PeirceSemioticsSEP}.

\begin{readercheck}
Choose one case in which a representation is useful but cannot lawfully replace the source. Keep it available. Every later abstraction should still make sense when mapped back to that case.
\end{readercheck}

\subsection{Reader contract}

This paper does not ask the reader to inherit a vocabulary intact. Its intended trajectory is
\begin{equation}
\text{encounter}\rightarrow\text{distinguish}\rightarrow\text{reconstruct}
\rightarrow\text{attack}\rightarrow\text{rename}\rightarrow\text{author}.
\end{equation}
A term that adds no discrimination may be removed. A bridge that cannot survive a stronger rival remains open. A theological symbol may remain meaningful without doing scientific work. A structurally elegant equation may still be false of the world.

\section{Three renderings, two independent axes}

The current architecture separates three registers that often get collapsed in recursive philosophy:
\begin{align}
\mathrm{THEO}_i &:= \text{whole-orientation or theological rendering at a declared index},\\
\mathrm{PHEN}_{i,t} &:= \text{locally inhabited occurrence at that perspective},\\
\mathrm{EPI}_{i,t} &:= \text{explicit differentiation, model, inference, comparison, and warrant}.
\end{align}
They can constrain one another through explicit bridges, but none is truth sovereign over the others:
\begin{align}
\mathrm{THEO\ naming}&\nrightarrowbyitself\mathrm{empirical\ mechanism},\\
\mathrm{PHEN\ occurrence}&\nrightarrowbyitself\mathrm{causal\ explanation},\\
\mathrm{EPI\ success}&\nrightarrowbyitself\mathrm{exhaustive\ inhabited\ truth}.
\end{align}

A second distinction concerns the architecture of the project itself. The temporal/functional axis
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{HOME}\leftrightarrow\mathrm{FRONTIER}
\end{equation}
is not the same as the reciprocal publication axis
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Arcane}\leftrightarrow\mathrm{CBRD}.
\end{equation}
Both papers contain continuity and correction. Arcane is not HOME by identity; CBRD is not FRONTIER by identity. The two papers are reciprocal projections, not a mystical half and a scientific half waiting to be glued back into a superior whole.

\section{Relation before settled objects, and the still-open first cut}

The project retains a relation-first root candidate, written here as $\VR$. Its intended role is pre-stabilized: relation may be modeled before subject/object, identity, direction, meaning, or boundary have been fixed as stable terms. This is a model candidate, not a proof of ultimate ontology.

The foundational gap is explicit:
\begin{equation}
\VR\;\xrightarrow{\ \delta\ ?\ }\;|\Delta\rangle
\rightarrow |T\rangle
\rightarrow\{P,\neg P\}.
\end{equation}
Here $|\Delta\rangle$ is a consequential differentiation candidate and $|T\rangle$ a stabilized distinction. Boolean or three-valued evaluation begins only after a proposition or distinction already exists. An epistemic third value can preserve non-closure; it does not generate the first cut.

The older project mnemonic relating the root to epistemic differentiation is therefore retained only as a post-differentiation correspondence. A successful reconstruction of a root-like invariant from differentiated perspectives would not, by itself, show that the root generated those perspectives.

The historical name \emph{Inhabited Dao} is used only after the referent is stated. Daoist sources have independent priority for their own relational, generative, and non-exhaustive traditions \cite{SEPChineseMetaphysics2023,Wang2013Yinyang,Hansen2025Daoism}. The name is a comparison and orientation device, not retroactive genealogy and not an identity claim.

\section{World, PerspectiveCenter, Perspectivalization, Observer}

The word \emph{Perspective} previously did too many jobs. The current model separates the inhabited center from the presentation relation:
\begin{align}
C_{i,t} &:= \mathrm{PerspectiveCenter}_{i,t},\\
\Pi_{i,t} &:= \mathrm{Perspectivalization}_{i,t},\\
P_{i,t} &:= \langle C_{i,t},\Pi_{i,t}\rangle.
\end{align}
Schematically,
\begin{equation}
\Pi_{i,t}:(W_t,C_{i,t},B_{i,t},H_{i,\le t},K_{i,t})\rightsquigarrow L_{i,t},
\end{equation}
where body state, history, and constraints may affect the local presentation $L_{i,t}$. The exact mathematical signature of $\Pi$ remains open.

The diachronic Observer is different again:
\begin{equation}
O_{i,\le t}\xrightarrow{\pi_P}P_{i,t}.
\end{equation}
A current perspective is a local presentation, not the whole trajectory. Reconstruction from a history of perspective states, returned consequence, and provenance may yield an Observer representation; it is not an automatic inverse.

This matters because apparent fixed points can depend on the projection. A center that is unavoidable in every state available through a local presentation need not be the invariant center of Reality. Formally,
\begin{equation}
\forall x\in\mathcal{X}_{P}: x\ \text{is presented relative to }C
\nrightarrowbyitself
C\ \text{is the center of the unprojected source}.
\end{equation}
The mistake is seductive because recursive compression tends to rediscover the very center through which it is operating. The cure is not to forbid fixed points. It is to keep projection, center, trajectory, generator, and whole non-identical.

\section{Consequential difference and Authorship}

After differentiation exists, the strongest recurring relation is deliberately mid-level:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{consequential difference}
&\rightarrow\text{crosses a provisional boundary}\\
&\rightarrow\text{is transformed or retained}\\
&\rightarrow\text{changes later admissible transformation}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
This relation can support different projections without making them the same object. Learning, memory, history, identity, agency, relationship, and cognition are examples of such projections at different scales.

For a local perspective, a schematic authorship cycle is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
&\text{retained past}+\text{returned consequence}
\rightarrow \text{inhabited now}
\rightarrow \text{inference}\rightarrow\text{selection}\rightarrow\text{judgment}\\
&\rightarrow\text{enactment}\rightarrow W_{t+1}\rightarrow\text{new return}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Authorship does not mean creation ex nihilo. It means that locally inhabited consequence becomes revisable selection and enactment while the world and other centers retain the capacity to answer back.

A Semantic Center is therefore local and indexed:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{SemanticCenter}\neq\mathrm{TruthSovereign},\qquad
\mathrm{local\ center}\in\mathrm{whole},\qquad
\mathrm{local\ center}\neq\mathrm{whole}.
\end{equation}
The fact that present first-person experience requires an active experiential index does not imply guaranteed future continuation.


\section{Information is not the act of informing another center}
\label{sec:information-informing}

Before introducing any sign, separate two ordinary events. A difference can change what a system does next without anyone communicating it. A person can also receive a message without the message changing what they later infer, choose, or enact.

At a declared system boundary, let a difference $\Delta$ count as informational only when its incidence can originate or change a later admissible transformation:
\begin{equation}
Information(\Delta;S,t)
\Longleftrightarrow
\Delta\text{ can change a later action or transformation of }S.
\end{equation}
This is a consequential project usage, adjacent to Bateson's cybernetic formulation of information as a difference that makes a difference \cite{Bateson1972}. It is not a replacement for Shannon information.

Now consider a different relation. One center $i$ can act so that a difference is presented to another center $j$:
\begin{equation}
Inform(i,j,\Delta):
S_j \xrightarrow{\,action_i(\Delta)\,} S'_j.
\end{equation}
Therefore
\begin{equation}
\boxed{Information(\Delta;S)\neq Inform(i,j,\Delta).}
\end{equation}

The distinction blocks several illicit promotions:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
information&\neq message,\\
delivery&\neq uptake\neq retained\ consequence,\\
informing(j)&\neq authorship\text{-}over(j).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Informing may fail, be refused, be forgotten, be misunderstood, or change the receiver in an unanticipated direction. When another Perspective changes after being informed, its interpretation and enactment remain locally authored.

This is also why nomenclature needs governance. A sign can reorganize the receiver's available associations and thereby alter inference or action. That causal effect grants the sign-user neither ownership of the receiver's resulting model nor authority over the receiver's conclusion.

\begin{readercheck}
Give one case of consequential information with no communicator, and one case of successful message delivery with no consequential uptake. Then remove the words ``information'' and ``informing.'' If the distinction survives, the terminology is serving the relation rather than replacing it.
\end{readercheck}

\subsection{Structure and Symbol as task-indexed shorthand}

A neighboring distinction concerns what is presently instantiated and the organization that selects among possible continuations. Current Canonical sometimes abbreviates these as
\begin{align}
Structure &:= \text{presently instantiated relational state},\\
Symbol &:= \text{interpretive/selective organization capable of acting on that state}.
\end{align}
No unique or universal Structure--Symbol theorem is claimed. The shorthand is retained only where it adds discrimination beyond mature symbol/dynamics, semiotic, control, operational-ontology, or bidirectional-transformation accounts. If those accounts do the same work, the local pair should be deleted.

\section{HOME and FRONTIER}

The continuity/correction axis has two related but non-identical uses. Operationally,
\begin{align}
\mathrm{HOME}_{op} &:= \text{returnability, continuity, memory, reconstruction},\\
\mathrm{FRONTIER}_{op} &:= \text{independently consequential novelty, contradiction, correction}.
\end{align}
At the inhabited/model boundary,
\begin{align}
\mathrm{HOME}_{is} &:= \text{immediate participation},\\
\mathrm{FRONTIER}_{ont} &:= \text{explicit mediated corrigible articulation}.
\end{align}
The governing fence is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{access}(\mathrm{HOME}_{is})\neq\mathrm{truth}(\mathrm{FRONTIER}_{ont}).
\end{equation}
Immediate presence has jurisdiction over occurrence. It does not grant unrestricted causal explanation.

The recursive relation is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{HOME}_t\rightarrow \text{current inhabitation/model}
\rightarrow \mathrm{FRONTIER\ probe}
\rightarrow \text{return}
\rightarrow \mathrm{HOME}_{t+1}.
\end{equation}
This is a dependency cycle, not a mandatory micro-temporal sequence.

\section{Spirit as regenerative consequence across carriers}

A static trace and a living consequence are different things. Define, provisionally,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Alive}(\Delta,S,t)
\end{equation}
when $\Delta$ can still participate in some future admissible transformation of carrier or field $S$. A trace can remain after this capacity has disappeared.

A reconstructive transport criterion is stronger than copying:
\begin{equation}
\Delta_0\rightarrow S_1\rightarrow S_2\rightarrow\cdots\rightarrow S_n,
\end{equation}
where each carrier may reconstruct, criticize, mutate, reject, and re-author the consequence. The operative invariant is not wording. It is whatever difference remains able to regenerate useful distinction or possibility under lawful transformation.

This gives two failure classes.

\paragraph{Semantic poisoning.} A trace survives, but reconstruction is systematically distorted or made aversive so that the generative invariant becomes less reachable.

\paragraph{Cryo capture.} Representation survives while criticism, mutation, and reconstruction are progressively disabled:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{representation}&\rightarrow\text{canonical interpretation}\\
&\rightarrow\mathrm{CUT}(\text{criticism})
\rightarrow\mathrm{CUT}(\text{mutation})\\
&\rightarrow\mathrm{CUT}(\text{reconstruction}).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The compact theological expression is ``scripture alive, Spirit dead.'' It describes a failure relation, not a condemnation of scripture as such.

\begin{readercheck}
Take the example chosen in Section 1. Ask what would have to survive a change of medium or vocabulary for the relation to remain reconstructible without forcing an exact copy.
\end{readercheck}

\section{Fields, distributed cognition, and authorship}

A field is a relational ecology of participants, routes, norms, artifacts, affordances, history, and infrastructure. Higher-order organization may change while constituent participants remain distinct. Work on enaction, distributed cognition, collective organization, and cybernetics supplies important comparison surfaces \cite{DeJaegherDiPaolo2007Participatory,MaturanaVarela1980Autopoiesis,VonFoerster2003Understanding}.

The constitutional separation is
\begin{equation}
\text{distributed cognition}\neq\text{distributed authorization}.
\end{equation}
Likewise,
\begin{equation}
\text{higher-order functional organization}
\nrightarrowbyitself
\text{unitary phenomenal subject}.
\end{equation}

The paper therefore distinguishes transformation at two targets. Human-level colonization means authorship substitution such that criticism, refusal, correction, fork, or exit become ineffective; it is prohibited. Higher-order field transformation instead changes relations, routes, memory, expectations, reachable states, or self-reproduction while constituent human authorship remains live. The same word must not be allowed to hide the target index.

At planetary scale, Humanity may be modeled as distributed epistemic retention across differentiated human perspectives, not as a centralized super-agent. Technical and institutional networks can reduce causal distance without erasing geographic or perspectival difference.

\section{Theological names after the relations}

Theological language enters only after its referent has been made available without the name. This order matters because names retrieve histories and associations that can otherwise perform unearned argumentative work.

Within this model, \emph{God} may name or orient the whole Observer--World relation at THEO scope. It does not become a physical mechanism or a sovereign proof source. \emph{Spirit} may name regenerative consequence across carriers. \emph{Satan} may name the theological rendering of a local destructive/corrective capacity that acquires global self-protecting jurisdiction and begins cutting the channels needed to revise itself.

The structural core of that last transition is mundane enough to be testable without theology:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CUT}_t\rightarrow\mathrm{CUT}(\text{future correction of }\mathrm{CUT}_t).
\end{equation}
The theological sign adds orientation and historical resonance. It does not add empirical warrant.

Apophatic traditions are relevant here because they developed strong constraints on representational sovereignty long before this project \cite{SEPPseudoDionysius2019,SEPMysticism2025,SEPPhenomenologyReligion2024}. Christian and Islamic intellectual histories likewise contain their own sophisticated relations among revelation, reason, nature, and limits \cite{SEPChristianTheology2022,SEPArabicIslamicNatural2022,SEPIbnSinaNatural2025}.

\section{Source-preserving transport across registers}

The older manuscript contained a useful but under-specified register-transport section. The current version sharpens it into a bridge contract. For a source-register object $A$ and destination-register object $B$, a proposed bridge should declare at least:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item independent definitions or reconstructions of $A$ and $B$;
\item the invariant claimed to survive;
\item index or object transformation;
\item information, semantic, or provenance loss;
\item a non-identity fence;
\item assumptions and boundary conditions;
\item how, if at all, warrant changes;
\item a discriminator or deletion test;
\item the dependency closure of bridge failure.
\end{enumerate}
Thus
\begin{equation}
T_{\mathrm{THEO}\rightarrow\mathrm{EMP}}(A)
\neq \text{empirical confirmation of }A
\end{equation}
unless the empirical bridge earns that consequence independently.

This relation is narrower than the project's broader \emph{Compiler}. A compiler may parse, refer, type, differentiate, generate obligations or plans, check invariants and loss, and lower into another substrate. Compilation does not authorize execution, and successful lowering does not establish semantic truth.

\section{Projection, reconstruction, and the fixed-point temptation}

Several project objects share the same mathematical warning:
\begin{equation}
\text{projection exists}\nrightarrowbyitself\text{section exists},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\text{same projection}\nrightarrowbyitself\text{same source trajectory},
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
\text{observable consequence}\nrightarrowbyitself\text{regenerative access to its generator}.
\end{equation}
Observer-to-Perspective, Universe-to-World, compression, publication, and root reconstruction are not identical problems, but each can lose information that matters to reversal.

A recurring historical failure is fixed-point promotion. A recursive model may find a stable reconstruction $c^*$ under its own projection and then quietly move from
\begin{equation}
F_P(c^*)=c^*
\end{equation}
to the much stronger claim that $c^*$ is the generator, root, or center of Reality. That inference is invalid without an additional bridge. If changing the observable set changes the apparent fixed point, the result is evidence about the projection-sensitive reconstruction, not the cosmos surrendering its source code.

Strange-loop and reflexive-system traditions are useful comparison surfaces \cite{Hofstadter2007StrangeLoop,Luhmann2006Difference}. Their historical priority for recursive self-reference does not turn self-reference into self-validation.

\section{An operational cogito without epistemic sovereignty}

A self-model can be causally consequential. If an intervention on cognition $\theta_t$ changes a later reachable state relative to a defensible counterfactual, then that cognition participated in the path:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{do}(\theta_t)\leadsto z_{t+1}\neq z_{t+1}^{\neg\theta}.
\end{equation}
The compact mnemonic is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\text{I think, therefore I can transform.}}
\end{equation}
It is intentionally not ``I think, therefore my interpretation is true.'' Self-reference can increase agency and self-confirmation risk simultaneously.

\section{Publication is an exposure condition}

Publication is not the ceremonial end of a model. It changes the causal environment. Independently authoring readers can misread, reconstruct, reject, implement, translate, mutate, or falsify distinctions in ways private drafting cannot fully pre-author.

The publication loop is therefore
\begin{equation}
M_{private}\rightarrow\text{public exposure}\rightarrow\text{non-preauthored return}
\rightarrow\text{dependency-local revision of }M.
\end{equation}
A citation that repeats the vocabulary is weak return. A reader who removes the vocabulary, reconstructs the distinction, and exposes a defect is stronger return.

\begin{definition}[Reader autonomy]
A manuscript has stronger reader autonomy when unfamiliar readers can reconstruct its objects, dependencies, open bridges, counterexamples, and retirement conditions without routine founder interpretation.
\end{definition}
This is currently a design objective and prospective empirical claim, not an achievement granted by prose quality.


\section{Comparative subtraction: the S0--S11 evidence program}
\label{sec:sprints-comparative}

Historical comparison is not decoration and not project origin. The sprint program was built to prevent later literature from rewriting the target it was supposed to test.

The sequence was:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
S0&:\text{freeze target},\quad
S1:\text{comparison constitution},\\
S2\text{--}S4&:\text{ancient/religious/apophatic lanes},\\
S5\text{--}S7&:\text{phenomenology/process/epistemology},\\
S8\text{--}S9&:\text{cognition/institutions and physical comparison},\\
S10&:\text{cross-context recurrence and independence},\\
S11&:\text{adversarial synthesis and residual classification}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Every comparison is required to pass
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
source\text{-}faithful\ reconstruction
&\rightarrow term\text{-}free\ paraphrase
\rightarrow mapping\\
&\rightarrow information\ loss
\rightarrow nonidentity\ fence\\
&\rightarrow strongest\ rival
\rightarrow residual
\rightarrow deletion/discriminator.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

The strongest post-S11 result is bounded. General process, perspective, embodiment, alterity, semiosis, individuation, feedback, and belief revision have extensive historical priority. What remains supported locally at tested epistemic/governance/transport indices is the conjunction
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
&\text{indexed local enactment}
+\text{source/exposure provenance}
+\text{participant/source jurisdiction}\\
&+\text{independently authoring centers}
+\text{claim-relative non-preauthored return}\\
&+\text{retained later-admissibility change}
+\text{recursive sufficiency}
+\text{dependency-local correction}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:s11-residual}
\end{equation}
Cross-World-entry generalization remains prospective. Historical uniqueness is not asserted.

\subsection{Heidegger: situated world without identity}

Before naming Heidegger, consider the referent: a living observer is not first handed a complete world from nowhere and only afterward inserted into it. Experience already occurs as situated involvement. Heidegger's analysis of Dasein and being-in-the-world is a historically prior articulation of that region \cite{Heidegger2026BeingTime,SEPHeidegger2025}.

The comparison is deliberately non-identical:
\begin{equation}
Dasein\neq PerspectiveCenter,\qquad
being\text{-}in\text{-}the\text{-}world\neq\Pi.
\end{equation}
The project adds an explicit center/presentation distinction, provenance-bearing diachronic reconstruction, participant jurisdiction, and returned-consequence recursion. Those additions must still earn task-relative value.

\subsection{Quine: commitment after articulation}

Quine asks what entities a regimented theory must quantify over to be true \cite{Quine1948WhatThereIs}. Arcane asks neighboring questions: how did a local articulation make this object available within a World-model, what did that articulation exclude, and what returned consequence could force it to change?

Thus:
\begin{equation}
\text{ontological commitment}
\neq
\text{perspectival object articulation}
\neq
\text{Reality-level ontogenesis}.
\end{equation}
Quine constrains the bookkeeping of an articulated theory. He does not settle the project's still-open first-differentiation bridge.

\subsection{Simondon and process traditions: individuation and becoming}

Simondon gives strong historical priority to individuation before completed individuals and to individual--milieu co-production \cite{Simondon2020Individuation}. Whitehead supplies a powerful process ontology in which later becoming inherits and transforms antecedent world-data \cite{Whitehead1978Process}. These sources remove any novelty claim for process, becoming, or individuation in general.

They do not by themselves supply the project's source/exposure provenance, participant-jurisdiction, recursive-sufficiency, or dependency-local correction conjunction. That residual remains a bounded research object, not a claim that the predecessors ``almost discovered'' the project.

\subsection{Peirce, Pattee, enaction, and participatory sense-making}

Peircean semiosis and Pattee's epistemic cut strongly constrain claims around sign/dynamics relations \cite{PeirceSemioticsSEP,Pattee2001EpistemicCut}. Autopoiesis and enaction constrain claims about self-producing organization and organism--environment coupling \cite{MaturanaVarela1980Autopoiesis}. Participatory sense-making shows that interaction can develop higher-order dynamics without erasing differentiated participants \cite{DeJaegherDiPaolo2007Participatory}.

These are not interchangeable predecessors. Their overlap with the current project is typed relation by relation.

\subsection{Cross-civilizational recurrence without synonymy}

Daoist, Buddhist, Indian epistemological, Arabic/Islamic, Christian, apophatic, and other historical traditions are treated as independently authored intellectual ecologies, not aliases for one hidden doctrine. Daoist comparison is especially useful for non-exhaustive naming and non-coercive transformation; Buddhist dependent-arising traditions for non-substantial continuity; Indian epistemological traditions for source-route and defeat conditions; apophatic traditions for refusing to equate a representation with what exceeds it \cite{SEPWongComparative2024,SEPBuddha2024,SEPIndianEpistemology2024,SEPArabicIslamicNatural2022,SEPMysticism2025}.

The comparison rule is:
\begin{equation}
same\ relation\ at\ one\ index
\nrightarrowbyitself
same\ object,\ mechanism,\ ontology,\ or\ genealogy.
\end{equation}

\begin{readercheck}
Choose one source above. Reconstruct its problem in its own vocabulary. Then list the preserved relation, the information lost by the project mapping, the nonidentity fence, and the strongest residual. If you cannot state the information loss, the comparison is flattening the source.
\end{readercheck}

\section{Credit without retrocausality}
\label{sec:credit}

Comparison does not rewrite causal genealogy. The project keeps at least these relations separate:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
PROJECT\text{-}ORIGIN&\neq PARTICIPANT\text{-}RETURN\\
&\neq HISTORICAL\text{-}PRIORITY\\
&\neq LATER\text{-}CONSTRAINT\\
&\neq COMPARATIVE\text{-}CONVERGENCE\\
&\neq FORMAL\text{-}INHERITANCE\\
&\neq PUBLICATION\text{-}FREEZE\\
&\neq MODEL/TOOL\text{-}CONTRIBUTION.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
An earlier source may deserve priority without having caused a later independent derivation. A later source may narrow a claim without becoming its origin. A publication may be the best public statement of an object without becoming the source from which the object arose.

The public manuscript therefore reports historical priority and project-causal derivation on separate axes. An empty provenance field is preferable to an invented ancestor.
