\section{Scope and non-claims}

CBRD is a model and research program, not a final theory of Reality. It starts after enough differentiation exists to state a claim, represent a state, or define a transformation. The project does retain a separate foundational question about first consequential differentiation, but no software object, truth value, qutrit, compiler stage, or notation is allowed to smuggle that differentiation into its own premises and then announce victory.

The basic fences are
\begin{equation}
\text{model}\neq\text{Reality},\quad
\text{formal consistency}\neq\text{empirical truth},\quad
\text{build success}\neq\text{claim truth},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\text{proposal}\neq\text{authorization}\neq\text{execution}\neq\text{verification}.
\end{equation}

CBRD is the epistemic--formal--operational projection of a larger endogenous research genealogy. Its companion \AM manuscript is the inhabited THEO--PHEN $\leftrightarrow$ corrigible-ontological-articulation projection. Neither paper is a wrapper or truth-sovereign parent of the other, and neither lends the other warrant by proximity. A reader should be able to assess CBRD without accepting Arcane terminology.

\section{Methodological constitution: disciplined incompleteness}

The methodological stance combines decomposition, source tracing, rivalry preservation, systematic definition, evidential caution, and jurisdictional limits. Descartes, Bacon, Locke, Bayle, Spinoza, Wolff, Hume, Kant, and Quine represent different historical components of this discipline rather than a fictional unified school \cite{Descartes1641,Bacon2000NewOrganon,Locke1975Essay,Bayle1991Dictionary,Spinoza1996Ethics,Wolff1730Ontologia,Hume1748,Kant1781,Quine1948WhatThereIs}.

The operational rule is simpler than the pedigree:
\begin{enumerate}
\item identify the object and frame before assigning status;
\item state where exclusion entered;
\item preserve alternatives when the premises do not decide among them;
\item separate source, representation, authority, and evidence;
\item require a target-specific bridge before moving a claim across registers;
\item let returned consequence revise only what actually depended on the tested object.
\end{enumerate}

The framework uses the phrase \emph{undetermined within the current frame} for ordinary scoped underdetermination. It does not turn every unresolved bridge into a Gödel or Turing theorem. The earlier manuscript's global ``Gödelian'' label is therefore retired. Formal incompleteness results remain relevant only where their premises are actually instantiated.

\section{Adversarial prior art: subtraction before residual claims}
\label{sec:prior-art}

Nearly every intuitive component of \CBRD has substantial ancestry. Prior-art subtraction must occur before any residual novelty claim is promoted beyond research-hypothesis status. Organizationally, Beer's Viable System Model is a serious predecessor for recursively differentiated regulation, and Hayes-Roth's blackboard architecture is a serious predecessor for shared integration/control surfaces \citep{Beer1984VSM,HayesRoth1985}. Performative prediction directly establishes that deployed predictors can alter the future data distribution they subsequently face \citep{PerdomoEtAl2020}. W3C PROV provides an established vocabulary for provenance entities, activities, and agents \citep{W3CProvDM2013}. These are inherited constraints, not footnotes around a supposedly isolated architecture.

\subsection{Symbols, semantics, and dynamics}

Pattee's work explicitly addresses the relation between symbolic descriptions and rate-dependent dynamics, using the ``epistemic cut'' and control/measurement relations to bridge them \citep{Pattee2001}. Brier's cybersemiotics is a transdisciplinary attempt to integrate cybernetics, semiotics, cognition, meaning, and phenomenology \citep{Brier2008}. Deacon develops a constraint-centered account of teleodynamics, function, and emergent normativity \citep{Deacon2011,GarciaDeacon2024}. Consequently, the statement ``meaning must be coupled to dynamics'' is not available as a novelty claim.

\subsection{Geometry and dynamics of meaning}

G{\"a}rdenfors developed conceptual-space approaches to the geometry of thought and meaning long before the present proposal \citep{Gardenfors2000,Gardenfors2014}, and more recent work explicitly addresses the geometry and dynamics of meaning \citep{Gardenfors2025}. Transformer-era work explores formal semantic geometry and latent semantic manifolds \citep{ZhangEtAl2022SemanticGeometry,Mabrok2026,HuNiuVarma2026,deZarzaEtAl2026}. Accordingly, \CBRD does not claim that semantic state is first recognized here as geometric or dynamic.

\subsection{History, causal states, and task-relative macrostates}

Computational mechanics builds minimal predictive causal states from histories with equivalent futures \citep{CrutchfieldYoung1989,RupeCrutchfield2024}. Flack treats useful macrostates as endogenous computational variables with downward consequence \citep{Flack2017}; Walker makes causal history part of scientific description \citep{Walker2014,SharmaEtAl2023}; causal-emergence work compares effective causal structure across scales \citep{HoelEtAl2013,Hoel2017}. Path dependence and transformation-relative coarse-graining are therefore established territories.

\subsection{Semantic information and viability}

Bateson's formulation of information as consequential difference is a direct cybernetic ancestor \citep{Bateson1972}. Kolchinsky and Wolpert give a formal intervention-based account of semantic information relative to viability \citep{KolchinskyWolpert2018}. Enactive and autonomy traditions similarly treat significance as relative to the self-maintaining organization of an agent \citep{MaturanaVarela1980,MorenoMossio2015}. The present paper therefore cannot claim invention of the idea that information becomes semantic by making a difference to an organized system.

\subsection{Belief revision and represented-versus-used information}

AGM formalizes belief revision \citep{AGM1985,Gardenfors1988}; dynamic epistemic logic studies information-driven state change \citep{vanDitmarschEtAl2007}; amnesic probing and causal abstraction separate decodability from causal use \citep{ElazarEtAl2021,GeigerEtAl2021,GeigerEtAl2023}; recent work shows strongly represented quantities that are causally inert to prediction \citep{LiaoCao2026}. Thus ``represented information need not be used'' is prior art. Any epistemic-torsion novelty must lie in its multi-interface, provenance-aware, governance-sensitive transport formulation.

\subsection{Open systems, agency, and composition}

Second-order cybernetics internalizes observer and feedback \citep{Wiener1948,vonFoerster2003}; autopoiesis and enaction model autonomous world-coupled agents \citep{MaturanaVarela1980,VarelaThompsonRosch1991,Thompson2007}; active inference formalizes nested boundaries, action, and updating \citep{Friston2010,KirchhoffEtAl2018,ParrPezzuloFriston2022}; categorical systems theory formalizes open systems and changing interfaces \citep{VagnerSpivakLerman2015,NiuSpivak2021,Spivak2026Interfaces,Spivak2026Dynamics}; Levin develops substrate-general multiscale cognition \citep{Levin2019,FieldsLevin2022,HartlEtAl2026}. These remain distinct predecessors that constrain the residual contribution.

\subsection{Causal abstraction, lossy projection, and writable representations}

Causal-abstraction work formalizes when interpretable high-level models are faithful simplifications of lower-level mechanisms, including soft interventions, arbitrary mechanism transformations, distributed alignments, and activation-steering style interventions \citep{GeigerEtAl2023,MassiddaEtAl2023,GeigerEtAl2024}. Xia and Bareinboim's projected abstractions explicitly address lossy representations in which distinct low-level interventions with different effects map to the same high-level intervention \citep{XiaBareinboim2025}. Consequently, CBRD cannot claim novelty for ``an abstraction erases intervention-relevant distinctions'' in general. Its residual burden is narrower: whether provenance/exposure/jurisdiction variables and self-action/update through the quotient yield discriminations not already captured by these abstraction formalisms.

Likewise, causal probing and activation steering already manipulate internal representations to test causal use \citep{DengEtAl2026,YeEtAl2026SteeringSignals}. A writable internal direction is therefore prior art, not anti-grammar novelty. Anti-grammar survives only if an explicit relational state induced by controlled semantic transformations provides transferable or corrective causal leverage beyond probing, causal abstraction, contrastive perturbation, and steering baselines.

\subsection{Behavioral perturbation and metamorphic testing}

CheckList, contrast sets, and metamorphic testing already use controlled input changes, invariances, and expected behavioral relations to diagnose model behavior \citep{RibeiroEtAl2020CheckList,GardnerEtAl2020Contrast,ChoEtAl2025Metamorphic}. Anti-grammar therefore cannot claim novelty for perturbing language while watching outputs. The residual candidate is the combination of task-relative relational-state induction, operational equivalence classes, a writable explicit state, and downstream causal tests.

\subsection{Residual novelty claim}

The accepted S0--S11 archaeology and directed literature review do not support a historical-uniqueness claim. They do leave a bounded residual research object at tested epistemic, governance, and transport indices:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item indexed local enactment;
  \item explicit source/exposure provenance;
  \item participant/source jurisdiction and independently authoring other centers;
  \item claim-relative non-preauthored returned consequence;
  \item retained later-admissibility change;
  \item recursive sufficiency under future transformations induced through use;
  \item dependency-local correction, failure localization, and non-annihilation across registers.
\end{enumerate}

This remains a bounded, task-indexed research hypothesis, not proof of historical uniqueness or universal irreducibility. A materially equivalent predecessor would revise the priority classification, not decide the substantive framework.

\section{Core relation and model-relative impossibility}

The strongest earned mid-level relation is
\begin{align}
\text{consequential difference}
&\rightarrow\text{crosses a provisional boundary}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow\text{is transformed or retained}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow\text{changes later admissible transformation}.
\end{align}
This pattern has substantial prior art in cybernetics, information, process, semiosis, and dynamical systems \cite{Wiener1948,Bateson1972,vonFoerster2003,Pattee2001,CrutchfieldYoung1989}. CBRD therefore does not claim invention of feedback, information, recursion, or process.

A model is written schematically as
\begin{equation}
M=(V,D,C,T),
\end{equation}
with variables, domains, constraints, and transition/search dynamics. Then
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Impossible}(s\mid M)\nrightarrowbyitself\mathrm{Impossible}(s\mid\mathrm{Reality}),
\end{equation}
and likewise for generator omission or agent-relative unreachability. The point is not that every excluded state is possible. The point is to localize where the exclusion entered before promoting it into an ontological conclusion.

A useful debugging procedure is:
\begin{align}
M&\rightarrow\text{excluded state}\rightarrow\text{locate exclusion}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow\text{audit variables/domains/constraints/transitions}\nonumber\\
&\rightarrow\text{revise or retain exclusion}.
\end{align}
