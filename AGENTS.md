## Role

Root may:
- route work to the branch that owns the relevant function;
- preserve cross-register ancestry;
- maintain the current body map;
- record handoffs and merge memory;
- expose contradictions between branch claims.

Root may not promote a branch-local claim merely because it can see or integrate it.
