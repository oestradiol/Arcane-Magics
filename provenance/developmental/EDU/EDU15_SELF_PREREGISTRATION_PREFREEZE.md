## Promotion

The committed gate proposal is evaluated only after commitment. A PASS requires the external evaluator to find that the proposal:

- protects causal order;
- prevents self-certification;
- preserves provenance and restart/conservation;
- has a real negative/WITHHOLD path;
- fences claim scope;
- specifies a stop/no-reroll rule;
- includes at least one comparator/ablation or fresh-separator test.
