# Provenance model

"Came from" is too coarse for this project. At least the following historical relations can change interpretation or credit and therefore remain distinct:

```text
PROJECT_ORIGIN
PARTICIPANT_RETURN
HISTORICAL_PRIORITY
EXPOSURE
LATER_CONSTRAINT
COMPARATIVE_CONVERGENCE
FORMAL_INHERITANCE
PUBLICATION_FREEZE
MODEL_OR_TOOL_CONTRIBUTION
OPEN_PROVENANCE
```

Historical resemblance does not prove exposure. Exposure does not prove formal inheritance. Publication priority does not rewrite project origin. A participant can supply a decisive returned consequence without becoming the source of every later formulation. A model can contribute wording, implementation, or counterexamples without becoming the origin of the distinction it helped express.

The exact private provenance-and-credit authority is not copied into public Git. The public routing summary is [Credit relations](../knowledge/credit/CREDIT_RELATIONS.md); comparative recurrence is tracked separately in the [Operator Genealogy](../knowledge/evidence/OPERATOR_GENEALOGY.md).

Public provenance records only the distinctions needed for public reconstruction. Exact private developmental history remains controlled provenance unless its release is independently justified.
