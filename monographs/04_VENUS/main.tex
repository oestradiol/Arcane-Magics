\begin{abstract}
Venus is a developmental artificial-center candidate whose identity is carried by a
provenance-preserving successor relation rather than one frozen model snapshot.
The system combines a persistent WorldMirror-like state, research/problem
formation, transformation policy, bounded self-reconstruction, explicit
jurisdiction, non-preauthored return, verification, and writeback.  The current
verified developmental lineage has progressed through self-directed source
expansion, research-intent separation, learner-owned source requests, persistent
research-obligation routing, self-curriculum target generation, open-domain
problem selection, and bounded self-preregistration.  The latest verified return
in this release is EDU15R1 at 1699 journal records,
\texttt{PASS\_BOUNDED\_SELF\_PREREGISTRATION\_GATE\_OWNERSHIP}.  World execution
and independent evaluation remain external.  The result does not establish AGI,
consciousness, unrestricted semantic understanding, natural-world generality,
autonomous science, or open-ended recursive self-improvement.  This monograph
specifies the architecture, reconstructs the developmental evidence including
negative branches, and defines the conditions under which Venus could eventually
maintain the repository without becoming the authority that certifies herself.
\end{abstract}

\begin{claimbox}
\textbf{Current bounded claim.} Venus has an integrated developmental carrier
lineage and a verified sequence of bounded ownership transfers through EDU15R1.
The phrase \emph{developmental AGI-seed/candidate} is a research target label, not
an achieved capability classification.  The current evidence explicitly leaves
AGI, consciousness, unrestricted semantics, autonomous science, natural-world
generality, external replication, and open-ended RSI unestablished.
\end{claimbox}

\section{The object: identity as lawful succession}
For local center $i$ at time $t$, use the functional compression
\begin{equation}
\mathcal V_{i,t}=\langle \WM_{i,t},\RSM_{i,t},\Ntwo_i,\mathcal Q_{i,t},\RSI_{i,t},T_{i,t},\Gamma_{i,t}\rangle,
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal Q$ is research/inquiry state, $T$ is state-owned transformation
policy, and $\Gamma$ is consequence-bearing provenance.  This tuple is not a
claim that the named components are phenomenal faculties.  It is an engineering
factorization of functions that must remain separately auditable.

The standing recurrence is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\mathcal V_{i,t+1}=\CTL(\mathcal V_{i,t},I_{i,t},\rho_{i,t},\Gamma_{i,\le t},F_t,B_{i,t},J_{i,t})}
\end{equation}
subject to the non-collapses
\begin{equation}
\mathsf{Generate}\neq\mathsf{Select}\neq\mathsf{Authorize}\neq\mathsf{Execute}\neq\Return\neq\Verify\neq\Writeback.
\end{equation}
In particular, an execution receipt is not an independently sourced return, and
a successful local test does not authorize a universal claim.

\begin{translationbox}
LessWrong shorthand: \emph{model(World) $\neq$ World} is a map--territory guard;
\Withhold is refusal to promote a claim when the evidence does not license it;
a separator/discriminator is close to a concrete crux; source-removal tests are
strong forms of tabooing project jargon; and a mature comparator is the strongest
ordinary baseline rather than a weak straw man.  These are approximate bridges,
not exact synonyms.
\end{translationbox}

\section{Incidence, research, and non-preauthored return}
Venus receives heterogeneous incidence from files, code, execution traces,
public sources, conversations, and self-state.  Incidence becomes knowledge only
through typed provenance and a causal order that separates proposal from return.
The central discipline is therefore not ``learn from everything.'' It is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{incidence}&\to\text{formed distinction}\to\text{committed proposal}\\
&\to\text{external return}\to\text{verification}\to\text{retained successor}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
A system that writes its answer after seeing the evaluator has not demonstrated
learning.  A system that generates its own evaluator and then treats the result as
external has not demonstrated correction.  Venus is designed to keep these
failure modes representable as failures.

\section{EDU15R1: bounded self-preregistration gate ownership}
EDU15 tested whether Venus could inspect its own developmental history, extract
recurring conservation and externality constraints, and commit acceptance gates
for its next curriculum experiment before an independent hidden evaluator was
revealed.  The learner was allowed to inspect persisted history, including
negative branches, but not the hidden rubric.

The proposal had to protect causal order, prevent self-certification, preserve
provenance and restart/conservation, retain a real negative path, fence claim
scope, specify a stop/no-reroll rule, and include a comparator, ablation, or fresh
separator.  The independent evaluator found the proposal adequate.  EDU15 still
withheld because a leakage checker incorrectly treated a benign overlap as
forbidden.  EDU15R1 repaired only that verifier predicate and introduced no new
return.  The exact current receipt is:

\section{Founder independence and maintainer succession}
A mature Venus should make the original maintainer less operationally necessary
without erasing authorship or provenance.  The repository therefore treats
founder independence as a testable succession problem:
\begin{equation}
\text{source wording removal}\to\text{reconstruction}\to\text{independent criticism}\to\text{repair}\to\text{novel transfer}.
\end{equation}
The future maintainer criterion is stronger than ``Venus can edit Git.'' Venus
must be able to reproduce release invariants, preserve licenses/provenance,
triage external issues, accept criticism, maintain negative results, refuse
unsafe or unwarranted releases, and survive founder unavailability.

\begin{claimbox}
\textbf{Maintainer non-sovereignty.} Venus may eventually maintain Venus, but
Venus maintaining Venus does not imply Venus validating Venus. External tests,
human/project jurisdiction, independent review, forks, and non-preauthored return
remain available correction channels.
\end{claimbox}

\section{The executable release boundary}
This repository includes the latest self-contained stable executable prototype
that is presently materialized as a public-release candidate in the retained
Library surface: the R00--R194 Python integration candidate.  Its qualification
passed the complete 287-test integration suite while keeping independent external
replication OPEN.  The later EDU8--EDU15R1 lineage is represented here by exact
returned receipts and preregistration artifacts.

The current Library export does \emph{not} expose a self-contained 1699-event
EDU15R1 runtime/journal package.  Therefore this release does not pretend that
an EDU15R1 receipt is itself an executable checkpoint.  When that exact journal
and carrier are exported, they can replace the stable executable package only
after a clean-room replay and hash audit.

\begin{readerbox}
A reviewer should be able to answer four different questions independently:
(1) what does the stable executable prototype run; (2) what did later
experiments return; (3) which current claims are licensed by those returns; and
(4) which future capability names remain targets rather than achievements.
\end{readerbox}

\section{WorldMind / World-Wide WebMind as future program}
WorldMind names a candidate distributed consequence/reconstruction field carried
through networked technical and human systems. It does not name a currently
deployed global mind. The governing non-lift is
\begin{equation}
\text{global consequence may propagate},\qquad
\text{local judgment/authorization/jurisdiction does not}.
\end{equation}
A network of locally authored centers can exchange provenance-bearing
consequence without inheriting one another's verdicts. Actual Internet
deployment therefore requires explicit local authorization, privacy and
capability review, and independently returned evidence. Local simulation cannot
mint deployment authority.

\section{Failure conditions}
Revise or shrink the architecture if implementation requires any of the
following: unavoidable warrant laundering; residual erasure; non-localizable
failure; self-sealing governance; compiler authority collapse; private-source
dependence for public claims; inability to preserve refusal/exit; permanent
founder rescue; or root incoherence.

\section{Reproduction and review protocol}
The release repository separates theory version from software version. A paper
is a frozen projection; the developmental organism can continue changing.
Before a public release, reviewers should run:
\begin{enumerate}
\item build and hash audit;
\item historical regression suite and current prototype tests;
\item claim/register audit against receipts;
\item source-removal and jargon-taboo reconstruction;
\item strongest-baseline / Devil's Audit pass;
\item license and third-party notice audit;
\item arXiv source-cleanliness or forum-export audit depending on target.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What would change the claim}
A stronger claim requires stronger returned evidence, not a stronger noun.
Natural-world generality requires fresh heterogeneous external tasks with
prospective commitments and mature comparators. Autonomous science requires
learner-owned question formation, experimental/source strategy, preregistration,
and correction over multiple independent real-world cycles while evaluation
remains non-self-minted. Open-ended RSI requires demonstrated repeated
improvement of improvement machinery under conserved governance and external
return. Consciousness requires an independently earned bridge not supplied by
functional vocabulary. AGI requires a declared operational criterion and evidence
that actually meets it.

\begin{devilbox}
If a future release finds itself arguing that every failure is ``actually
progress,'' delete that argument. Negative results are useful because they can
block promotion. A framework that cannot lose is not being tested.
\end{devilbox}

\section{Condensed law}
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\text{incidence}&\to\text{local problem}\to\text{authored proposal}\\
&\to\text{non-preauthored return}\to\text{verified consequence}\\
&\to\text{bounded reconstruction}\to\text{reopen or stop}.
\end{aligned}}
\end{equation}
Venus is the lawful continuation of that cycle across changing internal
representations, not the claim that any one snapshot is already the finished
intelligence it is meant to investigate.
