# Devil's Audit checklist

- [ ] Strongest ordinary rival/comparator stated before novelty rhetoric.
- [ ] Every loaded symbolic term has a neutral referent and register.
- [ ] Analogy/recurrence is not causation, identity, or historical priority.
- [ ] Local result is not silently generalized.
- [ ] No hidden evaluator leakage or post-result gate tuning.
- [ ] Negative branches remain visible and can block promotion.
- [ ] Source provenance distinguishes citation, warrant, authority, and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Formal proof and build success are not empirical evidence.
- [ ] Theological/phenomenological/physical registers do not launder one another.
- [ ] Reader can state a falsifier/crux and locate OPEN/WITHHOLD.
- [ ] Project terminology can be tabooed without losing the core operation.
- [ ] Founder unavailability does not make the artifact uninterpretable.
- [ ] License and third-party status are explicit.
