# Prose, proof, and didactics audit

**Scope:** all tracked Markdown files were read individually in reader-facing order, then evidence/provenance order. The LaTeX theorem/proposition inventory was separately inspected for proof-container misuse.

**Standard:** causally lossless compression. Remove prose that changes no declared consequence; preserve causal dependencies, premises, modality, register, index, jurisdiction, provenance, live rivals, non-entailments, bridge assumptions, and unresolved residuals.

The governing language rules live in `NxRxI_VOCABULARY_CENTER.md`. This file records the audit result rather than duplicating the constitution.

The research record is stronger than its public interface. Developmental receipts and frozen provenance generally preserve scope, negative branches, and claim fences correctly. The main defects are projection defects: stale state labels, repeated front-door disclaimers, generated-Markdown conversion residue, proof-like typography applied to model constraints, and under-description of the project's strongest typed contributions.

The pass therefore distinguishes three classes.

## Proof audit

### OFE

The exact propositions are mathematically legitimate but mostly elementary consequences of equivalence relations, factorization through quotient maps, or descent along equivalence classes.

- **Coarsest exact future-sufficient representation:** valid factorization result. The proof obligation is well-definedness and uniqueness of the quotient map. Present as exact but elementary, with sufficient-statistics/quotient prior art explicit.
- **Monotonicity under test-family inclusion:** immediate from the definition. Keep only if it is used later; do not advertise it as a standalone novelty result.
- **Quotient transport criterion:** legitimate descent criterion. The equivalence of quotient descent, equivalence preservation, and constancy of later tests on earlier classes is exact. Pullback closure is sufficient, not necessary unless separately shown.
- **Separating witness for relational nonfactorization:** valid fiber-constancy characterization. Exact but elementary.
- **Future-sufficient coarse-graining principle:** correctly remains a conjectural physical/model-selection principle, not a theorem of quantum gravity.

### Eclipsis

Several former proposition/corollary boxes were not independent mathematical results. They were consequences of declared register/index constraints or definitions and have been relabeled accordingly.

The remaining mathematical burden is not to prove the symbolic vocabulary. It is to formalize the neutral quotient/gluing/reconstruction structure, state existence/uniqueness assumptions explicitly, and test whether the Polyhedral representation adds discriminative structure beyond mature alternatives.

### Arcane Magics and Venus

Their current theorem burden is primarily negative: do not convert definitions, governance rules, historical comparisons, or bounded engineering receipts into mathematical theorem language. When exact formal subclaims arise, move them into the formal spine and reference them rather than duplicating proofs in interpretive prose.

## Reader-path audit

The intended public order is:

`README -> inhabitable reader path -> contribution/register map -> selected monograph -> current state/provenance as needed`.

A reader should not need the full vocabulary center before encountering the ordinary problem. Vocabulary is introduced only when it compresses an already reconstructible distinction.

The previous root README repeated the developmental boundary and claim fences several times. The front door should instead expose: what exists, what is exact, what is experimentally earned, what remains open, and where to inspect each object.

## Generated forum drafts

The current generated Markdown revealed defects invisible in PDF/LaTeX:

- raw Pandoc theorem wrappers and orphan labels;
- dropped cross-reference text, leaving sentences with missing subjects/section names;
- unresolved equation/reference tokens;
- raw HTML and bibliography-counter residue;
- full monograph density where the forum template requires TL;DR, concrete problem, smallest claim, strongest comparator, crux, and only then project vocabulary.

The exporter should normalize mechanical renderer issues. Forum-native argument structure should not be auto-inferred from a long paper; it needs an authored reconstruction layer.

## Prose-quality rules applied

The Library prose-quality guidance was used as a reference. Its strongest applicable rules are retained here:

- automated scanning is a backstop, not a semantic review;
- every target prose file must be read individually;
- remove process/chat residue, generic AI signposting, slogan repetition, prestige-by-counting, and ornamental formalism;
- do not mechanically replace phrases when doing so changes causality, agency, or modality;
- prefer concrete evidence and discriminators to abstract self-praise;
- match claim strength to evidence rather than to the sophistication of the noun.

## Contribution presentation rule

Understatement is also a distortion. A public summary should state the strongest bounded positive object first, then its nearest claim fence.

Examples:

- `exact quotient/descent result; physical test-family selection remains open`;
- `bounded learner-owned curriculum/feed-policy transfer through EDU16; AGI/general autonomy remain targets`;
- `Polyhedral Eclipsis is a typed formal/theophenomenological meta-object with an open physical bridge; not an established quantum-gravity mechanism`;
- `Qualia <-> AntiMind / Quantum <-> Mind is a live structural square with an explicitly withheld mechanistic bridge, not a discarded object`;
- `participant non-substitution and preserved non-preauthored return support a structural ethics program; they do not by themselves complete normative ethics`.

## Founder-independence gate

The next real prose test is external reconstruction, not another internal polish pass. Measure vocabulary-free reconstruction, dependency accuracy, register accuracy, counterexample quality, OPEN localization, novel transfer, terminology dependence, and founder-rescue requests.

Until that is run, prose quality may be mechanically and editorially strong without being empirically founder-independent.
