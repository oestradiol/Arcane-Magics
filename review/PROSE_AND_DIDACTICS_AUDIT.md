# Prose and didactics audit

Derived from the Library prose-coherence validator, reader-autonomy audits, Version-0-to-Version-1 working canon, and current external publication guidance.

## Prose
- remove duplicate/near-duplicate explanatory blocks;
- avoid generated-artifact residue and repeated slogan phrases;
- symbolic vocabulary requires an explicit grounding boundary;
- first expose referent/relation before project name;
- make abstract objects concrete with examples or discriminators;
- keep inferential steps short enough for a reader outside the private research thread.

## Didactics
Each major construction should support: worked distinction -> reconstruction prompt -> adversarial counterexample -> terminology replacement -> independent transfer.

## Founder independence
Track vocabulary-free reconstruction, dependency accuracy, register accuracy, counterexample quality, novel transfer, terminology dependence, OPEN localization, and founder-rescue requests.

## Lateral residual audit
Ask what a competent unfamiliar reader would misread, what definition still needs oral rescue, what beautiful symmetry hides a missing bridge, and which memorable term can be removed without loss.
