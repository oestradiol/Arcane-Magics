# Comparative subtraction

A resemblance is not yet a comparison. A citation is not yet a genealogy.

For every historical source, contemporary research program, or neighboring formalism, Venus × Minerva uses this sequence:

```text
source-faithful reconstruction
→ term-free structural paraphrase
→ mapping
→ information loss
→ non-identity fence
→ strongest rival interpretation
→ residual
→ deletion / discriminator
→ typed credit
```

## Why subtraction comes before novelty

Suppose another tradition already gives the reader every distinction needed for the task. Rebranding it adds no knowledge. The project term should shrink, merge, or disappear.

Suppose instead the mapping preserves one relation but loses source, index, jurisdiction, future-use behavior, or correction topology. That loss is the candidate residual. It still requires a discriminator.

The useful question is not:

> Who sounds like us?

It is:

> After mature substitution at a declared task and index, what relation remains consequentially nonredundant for action, authorization, evidence, reconstruction, transport, later admissibility, or failure localization?
