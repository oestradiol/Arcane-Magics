# RGM operator archaeology

This node records a recovered internal predecessor without turning chronology into authority.

The relevant question is not "was RGM secretly the current theory?" It was not. The useful question is narrower:

> Which governance relations were already explicit in RGM, which current relations do they resemble, and what transmission between them is actually established?

## Historical object

The recovered mature RGM corpus presents the **Recursive Gradient Model** primarily as a cross-register claim-governance grammar. Its public burden is to prevent invalid upgrades among structural, operational, phenomenal, normative, symbolic/metaphysical, empirical, and formal claims.

A mature RGM claim crossing registers was expected to expose something close to:

```text
source register
→ target register
→ bridge premise
→ rival explanation
→ downgrade condition
→ decision consequence
```

If the framework could not pay that burden, the claim was supposed to narrow, split, translate, downgrade, be replaced, or be removed.

This is historically prior inside the project's intellectual trajectory to the current RegisterBridge and typed-claim machinery. It is not evidence that the current formal object was mechanically inherited from RGM.

## Surviving operator families

### Cross-register non-entailment

RGM explicitly blocked moves such as:

```text
structure -/-> awareness without additional conditions
awareness/access -/-> phenomenal inwardness
integration -/-> goodness
symbolic meaning -/-> empirical evidence
citation presence -/-> claim truth
formal notation -/-> measurement
```

Current Venus × Minerva sharpens this family through typed registers, warrant, jurisdiction, provenance, bridge obligations, and dependency-local failure.

### Inference cost

RGM required a claim to "pay" for movement across domains. Current bridge machinery makes that intuition more explicit:

```text
historical inference cost
→ source/target typing
→ invariant + loss
→ warrant transformation
→ jurisdiction
→ discriminator
→ failure closure
```

The mapping is a same-role reconstruction, not a theorem of direct descent.

### Term removal and weakest sufficient vocabulary

RGM required strong terms to survive replacement by working parts and instructed the reader to prefer weaker language when it preserved the needed discrimination. It also allowed mature rival vocabularies to replace RGM locally when they did the same job with fewer assumptions.

This is a clear precursor to the current public rule:

```text
referent
→ relation
→ scope/index
→ sign
→ term
```

and to the sign-removal / reader-reconstruction test.

### Anti-self-sealing correction

RGM treated a framework that turns every objection into confirmation as failed governance. Serious pressure had to cause a visible local correction:

```text
narrow | split | translate | downgrade | replace | remove
```

Current machinery adds typed negative results, evidence-chain requirements, dependency-local update, and the distinction between a declared safeguard and one actually enforced by the transition graph.

### Formal authority limits

RGM's formal targets were governance safety and non-entailment rather than proof of metaphysical truth. Static formal artifacts were not allowed to impersonate native verification.

Current retype:

```text
formal consistency != empirical truth
compile/build success != semantic validity
capability != authority
verification route must match claim type
```

### Non-collapse of other centers

RGM's normative layer defined love as recognizing another center as real without collapsing its difference into self, model, use, fantasy, role, system, or sacred story. Its conflict procedure also preserved agency, dissent, exit, repair, and embodied costs against totalizing integration.

Current project language is sharper:

```text
Other != model(Other)
informing != authorship-over-receiver
higher-order organization -/-> merged participant authorship
```

RGM supplies an intellectual precursor to that non-collapse relation. It does **not** supply the later participant-return evidence from the post-David project history.

## What does not migrate

RGM also contained broader historical claims about awareness, experience, total process, unity, consciousness, and sacred language. Chronology does not promote those claims into current Canonical authority.

```text
historical RGM ontology -/-> current ontology
historical RGM consciousness definitions -/-> empirical bridge
historical symbolic totality -/-> physical mechanism
```

Only relations that survive current typing are retained here.
