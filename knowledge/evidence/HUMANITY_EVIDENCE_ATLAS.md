# Humanity Evidence Atlas

This atlas is a map of independent human attempts to solve neighboring problems. It is not a list of influences.

| Problem surface | Historical / contemporary neighbors | What they subtract |
|---|---|---|
| Situated world and embodiment | Husserl, [Heidegger](../comparisons/HEIDEGGER.md), Merleau-Ponty, Schutz | novelty of situated/embodied presentation |
| Historical interpretation and sedimentation | Gadamer, Ricoeur | novelty of historically mediated understanding |
| Alterity and independent correction | [Levinas, participatory sense-making, Longino](../comparisons/LEVINAS_PARTICIPATORY_LONGINO.md) | novelty of irreducible otherness / socially distributed correction |
| Process and successor conditioning | [Whitehead, Dewey](../comparisons/WHITEHEAD_DEWEY.md), Bergson | novelty of process and action-return in general |
| Individuation before completed individual | [Simondon](../comparisons/SIMONDON.md) | novelty of preindividual/individuation framing |
| Sign/dynamics coupling | [Peirce, Pattee](../comparisons/PEIRCE_PATTEE.md) | novelty of recursive semiosis and symbol/dynamics cut |
| Organizational closure and enaction | Maturana, Varela, Thompson | novelty of autonomy / organism-environment coupling |
| Observer-inclusive systems | second-order cybernetics, von Foerster | novelty of including observer in system description |
| Perspectival science | [Giere, Massimi](../comparisons/PERSPECTIVAL_REALISM.md) | novelty of situated scientific representation |
| Belief revision | AGM and dynamic epistemic traditions | novelty of revision as such |
| Compression / causal abstraction | computational mechanics, MDP abstraction, causal abstraction | novelty of task-relative abstraction |
| Information / viability | Shannon, cybernetics, semantic-information traditions | novelty of information semantics in general |

The atlas should grow by source reconstruction, not name accumulation. A new entry is useful only when it changes subtraction, comparison, or an OPEN bridge.

Each linked dossier reconstructs the source-side object, states the nonidentity, routes credit, and records the residual. Unlinked names remain atlas-level placeholders until a source-faithful dossier is written.
