# A world is locally available without being locally exhausted

A person never receives a complete Reality from nowhere. A room is first encountered as somewhere one can move, reach, remember, avoid, or use. A scientific instrument also presents a bounded transformation of its target rather than the target without mediation.

Two distinctions follow.

First, the history-bearing center is not the same thing as the relation by which a world becomes locally available to it.

Second, a current presentation is not the same thing as the center's diachronic history.

Only after those referents are clear does the project introduce:

```text
PerspectiveCenter_{i,t}
= inhabited / history-bearing local center

Π_{i,t} := Perspectivalization_{i,t}
= indexed presentation/differentiation relation

Perspective_{i,t}
:= <PerspectiveCenter_{i,t}, Π_{i,t}>
```

The exact formal type of `Π` remains open.

## Historical subtraction

Heidegger gives historical priority to a powerful account of situated being-in-the-world. Husserl and Merleau-Ponty constrain claims about intentionality, embodiment, temporal synthesis, and sedimented experience. Giere and Massimi provide scientific-perspectivist resources for situated representation without abandoning realism.

These sources remove any novelty claim for situatedness or perspective as such.

The narrower residual candidate combines:

```text
indexed local center
+ explicit presentation operator/relation
+ provenance-bearing diachronic reconstruction
+ participant/source jurisdiction
+ non-preauthored returned consequence
```

Whether that conjunction is useful beyond established formalisms is a task-indexed question, not a historical-uniqueness claim.
