# What can persist without remaining unchanged?

A river, organism, institution, relationship, and research program can remain recognizably continuous while their material state changes. Treating identity as unchanged substance makes that ordinary fact difficult to express.

A weaker starting point is:

```text
identity = continuity through retained change
```

This does not say every persistent pattern is one object. Provenance and the transformation class still matter.

The project's strongest mid-level relation is:

```text
consequential difference
→ crosses a provisional boundary
→ transformed / discriminated / retained
→ changes later admissibility or transformation
```

From this, several scale-relative compressions become available:

```text
learning = consequence changes future transformation
memory = persistence/reconstructibility of consequence
history = accumulated retained consequence
identity = continuity through retained change
agency = selectable/revisable continuation
relationship = mutually history-changing continuities
```

## Subtraction

Whitehead, Dewey, Bergson, Simondon, autopoiesis, and enaction all constrain any novelty claim around process, becoming, organism-environment coupling, or individuation before completed individuals.

The candidate residual is not "things change." It is the conjunction of retained consequential change with provenance, jurisdiction, recursively induced future dynamics, and dependency-local correction.
