# Alterity, participatory interaction, and criticism

Three neighboring traditions attack different versions of the same temptation: converting another center into an object already exhausted by one's own model.

## Levinas

Levinas gives historical priority to a philosophical treatment of alterity that resists totalization. The Other is not merely more content inside the same self-enclosed representation.

## Participatory sense-making

De Jaegher and Di Paolo show that interaction can develop autonomous coordination dynamics without collapsing the differentiated participants into one agent.

## Longino

Longino's social epistemology makes criticism from differently situated agents part of the conditions under which scientific communities can correct local bias.

## Project subtraction and residual

These traditions remove novelty claims for alterity, interactional emergence, or socially distributed correction in general.
