# Quine: ontological commitment is not ontogenesis

## Source-side problem

In *On What There Is*, Quine asks what entities a regimented theory is committed to. The canonical slogan concerns bound variables: a theory's quantificational form reveals what must be in its domain for the theory to be true.

## Term-free overlap

Both Quinean analysis and Venus × Minerva require discipline about what a representation says exists. Neither permits an object to enter a theory merely because a name sounds meaningful.

## Nonidentity

The project distinguishes three questions:

```text
what a regimented theory quantifies over
!= how a local model made an object/state admissible
!= how Reality itself differentiates
```

Quine is strongest on the first. The project audit also tracks where exclusion entered the representation and what returned consequence could force re-articulation.
