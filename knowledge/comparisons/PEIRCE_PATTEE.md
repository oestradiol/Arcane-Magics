# Peirce and Pattee: signs become consequential through interpretation and control

A sign is not important merely because it represents. It becomes consequential through relations of interpretation, control, measurement, and subsequent transformation.

Peirce gives historical priority to triadic semiosis and recursively generated interpretants. Pattee gives historical priority to the epistemic cut, symbol/dynamics relations, and semantic closure.

These sources sharply constrain any project claim that symbol/dynamics coupling or recursive semiosis is new.
