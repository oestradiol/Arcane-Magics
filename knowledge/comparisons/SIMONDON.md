# Simondon: individuation before completed individuals

## Source-side problem

Simondon treats the individual as a result of individuation rather than the primitive starting unit. Individuation unfolds from a metastable preindividual field and co-produces individual and milieu.

## What this subtracts

Any general claim that this project invented relation-before-completed-individual, individuation, or individual/milieu co-production is untenable.

```text
Simondonian preindividual != vacuous_relation
individuation != proof of the project's first distinction
milieu co-production != participant jurisdiction calculus
```

The project's first-differentiation arrow remains open. Simondon supplies historical priority and a serious rival vocabulary, not a solved bridge from the project's root candidate.
