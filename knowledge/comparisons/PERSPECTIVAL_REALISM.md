# Scientific perspectivism and perspectival realism

Scientific models are produced from particular epistemic positions, instruments, methods, and historical contexts. That need not imply that reality is whatever a perspective says it is.

Giere and Massimi provide mature ways to discuss perspectival science without collapsing into unrestricted relativism.

## What this subtracts

The project cannot claim novelty for:

```text
representation is situated
perspective != arbitrary fiction
multiple perspectives may constrain one reality
```

## Project-specific question

Venus × Minerva separates the history-bearing center from the presentation relation itself and asks how provenance, returned consequence, and model-guided action alter later presentations:

```text
PerspectiveCenter_{i,t} != Perspectivalization_{i,t}
```

Whether that decomposition is formally useful beyond existing perspectival frameworks remains open.
