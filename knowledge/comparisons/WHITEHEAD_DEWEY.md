# Whitehead and Dewey: process, action, and successor conditions

Whitehead and Dewey approach experience and reality through process rather than static substance. In different ways, both make action, inheritance, consequence, and ongoing reorganization central.

## What they subtract

They remove any plausible historical-uniqueness claim for:

- process ontology in general;
- identity through becoming rather than unchanged substance;
- action altering the conditions of later action;
- experience as temporally and practically organized.

## Nonidentity

The project does not identify Whiteheadian occasions or Deweyan transactions with its indexed Perspectives. Its residual candidate is narrower:

```text
retained consequence
+ explicit source/exposure provenance
+ jurisdiction
+ recursively induced future-use constraints
+ dependency-local correction
```

If process/pragmatist formalisms already recover those discriminations for a task, local vocabulary should disappear.
