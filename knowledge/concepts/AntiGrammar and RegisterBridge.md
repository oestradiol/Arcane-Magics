# AntiGrammar and RegisterBridge

`Grammar` is an indexed encoding. `AntiGrammar_T` is a consequential invariant or equivalence class under a declared transformation family `T`. `RegisterBridge` is governed transport between typed objects or registers with explicit invariant, transform, loss, nonidentity, provenance, jurisdiction, warrant, and failure closure.

AntiGrammar identifies what survives a transformation family. RegisterBridge governs a crossing. [[Compiler]] may use either, but is not identical to either.
