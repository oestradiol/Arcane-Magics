# HOME and FRONTIER

`HOME` and `FRONTIER` are projections over one history.

`HOME` emphasizes robust returnability, continuity, memory, and reconstructible supported state. `FRONTIER` emphasizes unresolved novelty, contradiction, criticism, experiments, and correction.

A claim moving from FRONTIER to HOME is a governed status transition, not a metaphysical relocation. See [[Warrant Trichotomy]], [[Governance Qutrit]], and [[Publication as Projection]].
