# Authorship and participant jurisdiction

Authorship is source-indexed and consequence-bearing. Another participant's interpretation, consent, refusal, action, or boundary cannot be replaced by a locally generated model of them.

```text
Other != model(Other)
influence != authorship
prediction != authorization
```

Participant rights such as criticism, correction, refusal, mutation, fork, and exit operate as constraints on lawful transformations at the relevant index.
