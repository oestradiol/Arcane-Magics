# Negative results and residuals

Failure to establish a claim is not empty output.

A residual retains enough structure to constrain later work: attempted route, assumptions, unresolved alternatives, missing premises/bridges, failure localization, what the result may update, what it may not update, and a reopening condition.

```text
UNDETERMINED_F(P) != WITHHOLD
WITHHOLD != false
negative result != deletion
```

This lets unresolved consequence change future admissibility without manufacturing Boolean closure.
