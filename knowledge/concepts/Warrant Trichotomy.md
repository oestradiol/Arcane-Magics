# Warrant trichotomy

For an already formed proposition `P` in frame `F`, the warrant layer distinguishes `LICENSE_F(P)`, `LICENSE_F(not-P)`, and `UNDETERMINED_F(P)`.

The third state records that the frame licenses neither Boolean conclusion at the required warrant. It is not a third ontic substance and is not the same relation as governance [[Governance Qutrit|WITHHOLD]].
