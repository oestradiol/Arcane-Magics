# Perspective Center and Perspectivalization

A **PerspectiveCenter** is an inhabited, history-bearing local center. **Perspectivalization** `Π_i` is the indexed presentation/differentiation relation through which a local World becomes available.

```text
PerspectiveCenter != Perspectivalization
current presentation != diachronic Observer
```

`Π_i` may select, filter, index, or stabilize access to already differentiated structure. It is not licensed as the source of first ontic differentiation.
