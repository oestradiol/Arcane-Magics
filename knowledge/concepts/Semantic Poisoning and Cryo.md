# Semantic poisoning and Cryo

A representation can persist while its generative/corrective relation dies.

```text
representation
→ canonical interpretation
→ CUT(criticism)
→ CUT(mutation)
→ CUT(reconstruction)
```

The result is **Cryo capture**: transmission survives in form while regenerative access is lost.

The empirical frontier distinguishes erasure, ordinary drift, hostile reinterpretation, aversion, and persistent trace that actively blocks reconstruction of the generative invariant.
