# Governance qutrit

Governance acts on an already typed candidate: `ADMIT | WITHHOLD | REJECT/CUT`.

`WITHHOLD` preserves a residual and a reopening surface. `REJECT/CUT` removes a route from current admissibility while retaining provenance and scoped grounds. `ADMIT` is task- and index-scoped authorization, not unrestricted truth.

See [[Warrant Trichotomy]] for the separate epistemic coordinate.
